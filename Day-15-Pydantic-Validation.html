Pydantic Validation, Field() & Rules

Simple notes for validating client data before it reaches your database.

Click any topic to open it.

What is Validation?

Validation checks whether the incoming client data is correct before processing or storing it.

Why do we use Field()?

Field() is used to apply validation rules and metadata to Pydantic model fields.

What does ... mean?

It indicates that the field is required.

Key Points
  • Validation protects the database from invalid data.
  • Field() is used to define validation rules.
  • ... makes a field required.
  • min_length and max_length are used for strings.
  • gt, ge, lt, and le are used for numeric values.
  • If validation fails, FastAPI automatically returns 422 Unprocessable Entity.
